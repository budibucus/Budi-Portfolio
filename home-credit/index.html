The challenge

When I joined Home Credit as Senior Product Manager, the app — running on top of a global platform of 50M+ users — was, in practice, a payment-tracking tool. People opened it when a financing obligation came due and closed it the moment that obligation was settled. Nothing invited them to stay.

That gap cut two ways. The obvious one was a missed opportunity: a large, captive, trusted audience, and no product surface turning that trust into a new revenue stream. The less visible one was already costing us — friction in the core financing journey itself, from application through scoring to approval, was capping completion rates, and fraud exposure kept climbing as the platform scaled.

What I did

A marketplace integrated with finance — not bolted on

The vision was to make shopping feel like a natural extension of the financing product users already relied on: personalized recommendations based on financing history, a streamlined checkout with financing baked in, and simple navigation. Consumer research — behavioral data from the existing app plus user surveys — became the backbone of the strategy, identifying what would actually drive engagement and purchase intent.

  • Integration with core offerings so users could apply financing they already understood directly to marketplace purchases — one seamless flow instead of two disconnected products.
  • Partnerships to expand the offering fast, including iPrice, letting us offer a wide product range without building an inventory system from scratch — a functioning ecosystem from day one.

Phased execution

  • Roadmap & MVP covering core browsing, checkout, and financing integration — launch fast, iterate on real usage data.
  • Testing & feedback loops — A/B testing once live, driving iteration on UI/UX, recommendations, and payment flows.
  • Scaling & expansion — layering in personalized recommendations, deeper financing integration, and expanded categories through partnerships, with investment in performance to handle growing traffic.
  • Marketing & user education — in-app notifications, email, and social campaigns to drive adoption.

Fixing the core financing journey

Alongside the marketplace, I owned the financing journey from application entry through scoring, decisioning, and approval. I built full-funnel tracking to pin down exactly where applicants dropped off, then launched anti-fraud workflows that combined behavioural data, biometrics, and third-party risk signals to catch risk earlier in the funnel.

The outcome

Marketplace

  • Launched a digital marketplace integrated with financing APIs, generating $500K GMV in the first year.
  • Daily active users up 20%; marketplace usage up 30%.
  • Financing applications up 25% — a direct ripple effect into the core business.

Digital financing journey

  • Application completion time reduced 40% through full-funnel tracking and targeted friction fixes.
  • Fraud losses decreased and conversion quality improved as the new anti-fraud workflows caught risk earlier — across a platform serving 50M+ users globally.
The takeaway. This wasn't just a marketplace build — it was a lesson in balancing an ambitious vision with an actionable, phased strategy, and turning that into a product that moved real business metrics rather than just shipping something flashy.
