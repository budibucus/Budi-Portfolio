## How I work

- **I ship from ambiguity.** Most of my roles started with "build something from nothing" — a marketplace, internal tooling for a scaling partner network, an AI automation pipeline in a regulated industry. I'm comfortable defining the problem before I define the solution.
- **I balance outcomes with process.** A metric like $1M GMV or claims cut from 2 months to 3 days only means something alongside how it was reached — the prioritization calls, the trade-offs, the things that didn't work first.
- **I build where compliance and user experience have to coexist.** From OJK-regulated fintech to AI features in regulated healthcare, I've learned to bring compliance teams in ahead of engineering work, not after.
