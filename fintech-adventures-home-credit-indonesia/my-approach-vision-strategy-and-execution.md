### Vision: Crafting a Unified Digital Experience

The vision was to transform Home Credit's app into a one-stop digital ecosystem — one where users could manage their financing, shop, and discover services in a single, connected experience. The goal wasn't to bolt on a marketplace; it was to make it feel like a natural extension of the financing product users already relied on.

Key elements of the vision:

* **A marketplace integrated with finance** — not a separate entity, but a feature that complemented Home Credit's core financing offering.
* **Enhanced user engagement** — turning passive, obligation-driven visits into active, exploratory ones.
* **Long-term growth** — positioning the marketplace as a new, scalable revenue stream.

### Strategy: Laying the Foundation for Success

1. **User-centric product development.** We dug into consumer research — behavioral data from the existing app plus user surveys — to identify what would actually drive engagement and purchase intent. That research became the backbone of the product strategy.
2. **Integration with core offerings.** The marketplace was designed so users could apply financing they already understood directly to marketplace purchases — one seamless flow instead of two disconnected products.
3. **Prioritizing high-impact features** over building everything at once: personalized product recommendations based on financing history, streamlined checkout with financing baked in, and simple, intuitive navigation.
4. **Partnerships to expand the offering fast.** Strategic partnerships (including iPrice) let us offer a wide product range without building an inventory system from scratch — a functioning ecosystem from day one.

### Execution: Turning Strategy Into Reality

1. **Phase 1 — Roadmap and MVP.** A clear roadmap with an MVP covering core browsing, checkout, and financing integration, so we could launch fast and iterate on real usage data.
2. **Phase 2 — Testing and feedback loops.** A/B testing and continuous feedback once live, driving iteration on UI/UX, recommendations, and payment flows.
3. **Phase 3 — Scaling and expansion.** Once the MVP was validated, we layered in personalized recommendations, deeper financing integration, and expanded categories through partnerships — while investing in performance to handle growing traffic.
4. **Phase 4 — Marketing and user education.** In-app notifications, email, and social campaigns to drive awareness and adoption of the new marketplace features.
