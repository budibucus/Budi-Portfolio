When I joined Home Credit as Senior Product Manager, the app — running on top of a global platform of 50M+ users — was, in practice, a payment-tracking tool. People opened it when a financing obligation came due and closed it the moment that obligation was settled. Nothing invited them to stay.

That gap cut two ways. The obvious one was a missed opportunity: a large, captive, trusted audience, and no product surface turning that trust into a new revenue stream. The less visible one was already costing us — friction in the core financing journey itself, from application through scoring to approval, was capping completion rates, and fraud exposure kept climbing as the platform scaled.

I took ownership of that financing journey end to end, from application entry through scoring, decisioning, and approval. I built full-funnel tracking to pin down exactly where applicants dropped off, then launched anti-fraud workflows that combined behavioural data, biometrics, and third-party risk signals to catch risk earlier in the funnel. In parallel, I set out to make the app worth coming back to: I researched what would actually drive engagement, then launched a marketplace tightly integrated with the financing customers already trusted — leaning on partnerships, iPrice among them, to widen product range fast without standing up inventory infrastructure of our own.

In its first year, the marketplace generated **$500K GMV**. Application completion time fell **40%**, and fraud losses dropped as the new workflows moved detection upstream. See [My Approach](my-approach-vision-strategy-and-execution/) and [The Results](the-results/) for the full breakdown.
