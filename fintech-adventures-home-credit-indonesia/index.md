**Problem:** Home Credit's app was a payment-tracking tool — useful, but users only opened it when they had a financing obligation to manage. There was no reason to engage day-to-day, and no product surface turning that captive user base into a new revenue stream. Meanwhile, the core digital financing journey itself had friction eating into completion rates, and fraud exposure was a growing risk.

**My role:** As Senior Product Manager, I owned the digital financing journey end to end — application entry through scoring, decisioning, and approval — and led the marketplace from concept to launch (see [My Approach](my-approach-vision-strategy-and-execution/)).

**Result:** Launched a digital marketplace integrated with financing APIs that generated **$500K GMV in its first year**. Cut application completion time by **40%** through full funnel tracking, and reduced fraud losses by launching anti-fraud workflows combining behavioural data, biometrics, and third-party risk signals. See [The Results](the-results/).
