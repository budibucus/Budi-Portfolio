**Situation:** Home Credit's app, serving a platform of 50M+ users globally, was purely a payment-tracking tool. Users opened it when they had a financing obligation to manage, and closed it the moment that obligation was handled. There was no reason to stay.

**Problem:** That left two problems stacked on top of each other. First, a missed opportunity: a large, captive, trusted user base with no product surface turning that trust into a new revenue stream. Second, a live one: friction in the core digital financing journey itself — from application to scoring to approval — was capping completion rates, and fraud exposure was a growing risk as the platform scaled.

**Action:** As Senior Product Manager, I owned the digital financing journey end to end — application entry through scoring, decisioning, and approval — building full funnel tracking to find exactly where users dropped off, and launching anti-fraud workflows that combined behavioural data, biometrics, and third-party risk signals. Alongside that, I took the app from a single-purpose utility to a one-stop ecosystem: researching what would actually drive engagement, then launching a marketplace tightly integrated with the financing users already trusted, backed by partnerships (including iPrice) to expand product range fast without building inventory infrastructure from scratch.

**Result:** The marketplace generated **$500K GMV in its first year**. Application completion time dropped **40%**, and fraud losses fell as the new anti-fraud workflows caught risk earlier in the funnel.
