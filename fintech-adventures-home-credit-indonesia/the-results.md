**Marketplace:**

* Launched a digital marketplace integrated with financing APIs, generating **$500K GMV in the first year**.
* Daily active users up **20%**; marketplace usage up **30%**.
* Financing applications up **25%** — a direct ripple effect into the core business.

**Digital financing journey:**

* Reduced application completion time by **40%**, by building full funnel tracking across the digital financing journey and identifying friction points.
* Decreased fraud losses and improved conversion quality by launching anti-fraud workflows combining behavioural data, biometrics, and third-party risk signals — across a platform serving 50M+ users globally.

**The takeaway:** this wasn't just a marketplace build — it was a lesson in balancing ambitious vision with an actionable, phased strategy, and turning that into a product that moved real business metrics rather than just shipping something flashy.
