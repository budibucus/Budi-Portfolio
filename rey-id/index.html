The challenge

By 2023, Rey.id had something most digital-health companies never earn: an established name in Indonesian healthcare and insurance, a real user base, and real trust. The product hadn't caught up to that trust. Doctor consultations and insurance claims were still slow and manual, and the platform was leaving revenue and efficiency on the table that its user base would happily have supported.

I joined as VP of Product to own the full roadmap across three verticals at once — telehealth, insurance, and medical data — none of which could wait for the others. Two years later, back as Product Consultant, the problem had narrowed to a sharp point: claims for a state-owned insurer still took two months each, and Rey.id had no mass-market channel to reach anyone beyond its existing base.

What I did

1 · Redesigning the user experience

We rebuilt the app's design and user flow around consumer research and real behaviour:

  • Simplified navigation to key services — doctor consultations, mental health support, insurance claims. First-response time for doctor chat dropped to under 5 minutes.
  • Mapped the full checkout funnel, identified the two highest drop-off screens, and rebuilt KYC document upload and payment error handling — reducing add-to-cart abandonment from 93% to 85%.
  • Integrated APIs with partner hospital and pharmacy systems for real-time fulfilment, lifting teleconsultation utilization 25% and cutting prescription turnaround from 110 to 75 minutes.

2 · Integrating AI to revolutionize insurance claims

The claims process was heavily manual, from invoice processing to document review. I led an AI/ML project to digitize it and reduce human intervention — later extended to a state-owned insurer processing 6,000 claims a month across 100K+ members:

  • Defined training data structure and validation criteria alongside the data engineering team for models analyzing and digitizing hospital invoices and documents.
  • Designed safety guardrails, escalation logic, and human-in-the-loop review workflows — essential for AI operating in a regulated healthcare context.
  • Continuous-learning algorithms that improved the AI's ability to handle more complex claims over time.

3 · Enterprise and partnership delivery

I owned end-to-end product definition for enterprise client onboarding — translating client policy rules and operational workflows into system requirements — and managed external stakeholders across telco partners, state-owned enterprise clients, and licensed medical practitioners. This included co-creating an embedded insurance product bundled into a major telco's mobile data packages, opening a mass-market distribution channel that hadn't existed for Rey.id before.

4 · Data-driven decision making

Every decision was backed by data — funnel instrumentation and analytics (Mixpanel) let the team iterate on real behavioural data rather than assumption, across usage patterns, feedback, and service efficiency.

The outcome

As VP of Product

  • Scaled the platform to 100K+ active users and $1M GMV within 12 months, across telehealth, insurance, and payments.
  • Conversion rates up from 0.01% to 1.5%.
  • App performance improved 30%, with faster response times across services.
  • Add-to-cart abandonment reduced from 93% to 85%; teleconsultation utilization up 25%; prescription turnaround cut from 110 to 75 minutes.

As Product Consultant

  • Led AI claims automation for a state-owned insurer — 6,000 claims/month, 100K+ members — reducing claim processing time from 2 months to under 3 days.
  • Co-created an embedded insurance product bundled into a major Indonesian telco's mobile data packages, onboarding 200,000+ new users through a mass-market channel that hadn't previously existed.
The takeaway. The two roles told a continuous story — first proving the platform could scale on strong fundamentals (UX, funnel discipline, partner integrations), then applying that same discipline to AI automation in a regulated environment where trust and accuracy couldn't be compromised for speed.
