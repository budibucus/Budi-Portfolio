**Situation:** By 2023, Rey.id was an established name in Indonesian digital healthcare and insurance — real user base, real trust. But the product hadn't caught up to that trust. Doctor consultations and insurance claims were still slow and manual, and the platform was leaving revenue and efficiency on the table that its user base could have supported.

**Problem:** I joined as VP of Product to own the full roadmap across three verticals at once — telehealth, insurance, and medical data — with no single one of them able to wait for the others. Two years later, as Product Consultant, the problem sharpened further: Rey.id's claims process for a state-owned insurer was still taking two months per claim, and the platform had no mass-market distribution channel to reach users beyond its existing base.

**Action:** As VP of Product, I rebuilt the checkout and consultation funnels around real user behavior — mapping drop-off points, rebuilding KYC and payment error handling, and integrating directly with partner hospital and pharmacy systems for real-time fulfilment. As Product Consultant, I shifted focus to AI: leading claims automation for a state-owned insurer, defining training data structure and validation criteria with the data engineering team, and designing the safety guardrails and human-in-the-loop review workflows that regulated healthcare AI requires. In parallel, I owned enterprise onboarding end-to-end and co-created an embedded insurance product bundled into a major telco's mobile data packages — a distribution channel that hadn't existed for Rey.id before.

**Result:** Scaled the platform to **100K+ active users and $1M GMV within 12 months**. Later, cut claim processing time from **2 months to under 3 days** across 6,000 claims/month and 100K+ members, and onboarded **200,000+ new users** through the telco partnership. See [My Approach](my-approach-a-user-centric-digital-transformation/) and [The Results](the-results-a-new-era-for-rey.id/) for the full breakdown.
