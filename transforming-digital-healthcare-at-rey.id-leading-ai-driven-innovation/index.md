By 2023, Rey.id had something most digital-health companies never earn: an established name in Indonesian healthcare and insurance, a real user base, and real trust. The product hadn't caught up to that trust. Doctor consultations and insurance claims were still slow and manual, and the platform was leaving revenue and efficiency on the table that its user base would happily have supported.

I joined as VP of Product to own the full roadmap across three verticals at once — telehealth, insurance, and medical data — none of which could wait for the others. Two years later, back as Product Consultant, the problem had narrowed to a sharp point: claims for a state-owned insurer still took two months each, and Rey.id had no mass-market channel to reach anyone beyond its existing base.

As VP of Product, I rebuilt the checkout and consultation funnels around how people actually behaved — mapping drop-off points, reworking KYC and payment error handling, and integrating directly with partner hospital and pharmacy systems for real-time fulfilment. As Product Consultant, I turned to AI: I led claims automation for the state-owned insurer, worked with the data engineering team to define training data structure and validation criteria, and designed the safety guardrails and human-in-the-loop review workflows that regulated healthcare AI requires. Alongside that, I ran enterprise onboarding end to end and co-created an embedded insurance product bundled into a major telco's mobile data packages — a distribution channel Rey.id had never had.

The platform scaled to **100K+ active users and $1M GMV within 12 months**. Claims processing later dropped from **2 months to under 3 days** across 6,000 claims a month and 100K+ members, and the telco partnership brought in **200,000+ new users**. See [My Approach](my-approach-a-user-centric-digital-transformation/) and [The Results](the-results-a-new-era-for-rey.id/) for the full breakdown.
