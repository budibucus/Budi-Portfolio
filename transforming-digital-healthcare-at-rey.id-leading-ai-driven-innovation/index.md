**Problem:** Rey.id was an established name in healthcare and insurance, but its digital experience hadn't kept pace — doctor consultations and insurance claims were still slow and manual. Users were engaged, but the product was leaving trust, revenue, and efficiency on the table.

**My role:** I led product for Rey.id across two roles. As **VP of Product** (2023–2024), I owned the full roadmap across telehealth, insurance, and medical data. As **Product Consultant** (2025), I led AI-powered automation initiatives for claims processing and enterprise onboarding, operating in a regulated healthcare context alongside compliance and data engineering teams. See [My Approach](my-approach-a-user-centric-digital-transformation/).

**Result:** Scaled the platform to **100K+ active users and $1M GMV within 12 months**. Later, led AI claims automation for a state-owned insurer covering **6,000 claims/month and 100K+ members**, cutting claim processing time from **2 months to under 3 days** — and co-created an embedded insurance product bundled into a major Indonesian telco's mobile data packages, onboarding **200,000+ new users** through a distribution channel that hadn't existed before. See [The Results](the-results-a-new-era-for-rey.id/).
