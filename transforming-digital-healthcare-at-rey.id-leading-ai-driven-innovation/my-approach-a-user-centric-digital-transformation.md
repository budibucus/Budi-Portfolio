I led the revamp of Rey.id's platform around three pillars: user-centric design, AI-driven automation, and a data-driven approach to service delivery — later extending into AI claims automation and enterprise partnerships as Product Consultant.

**1. Redesigning the user experience**

We rebuilt the app's design and user flow based on consumer research and feedback:

* **Simplified navigation** to key services — doctor consultations, mental health support, insurance claims.
* **Faster response times** — first-response time for doctor chat services dropped to under 5 minutes.
* Mapped the full checkout funnel, identified the two highest drop-off screens, and rebuilt KYC document upload and payment error handling — reducing add-to-cart abandonment from 93% to 85%.
* Integrated APIs with partner hospital and pharmacy systems for real-time fulfilment, increasing doctor teleconsultation utilization by 25% and cutting prescription turnaround from 110 to 75 minutes.

**2. Integrating AI to revolutionize insurance claims**

The claims process was ripe for disruption — heavily manual, from invoice processing to document review. I led an AI/ML project to digitize the claims process and reduce human intervention, later extending this to a state-owned insurer processing 6,000 claims/month across 100K+ members:

* **Defined training data structure and validation criteria** alongside the data engineering team for AI models analyzing and digitizing hospital invoices and documents.
* **Designed safety guardrails, escalation logic, and human-in-the-loop review workflows** — essential for AI features operating in a regulated healthcare context.
* **Continuous learning algorithms** that improved the AI's ability to handle more complex claims over time.

**3. Enterprise and partnership delivery**

Owned end-to-end product definition for enterprise client onboarding — translating client policy rules and operational workflows into system requirements — and managed external stakeholder relationships across telco partners, state-owned enterprise clients, and licensed medical practitioners. This included co-creating an embedded insurance product bundled into a major telco's mobile data packages, opening a mass-market distribution channel that hadn't existed for Rey.id before.

**4. Data-driven decision making**

Every decision was backed by data — funnel instrumentation and analytics (Mixpanel) let the team iterate on real behavioural data rather than assumption, across usage patterns, feedback, and service efficiency.
