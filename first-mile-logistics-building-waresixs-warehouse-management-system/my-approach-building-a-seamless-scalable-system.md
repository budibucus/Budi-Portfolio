**1. Internal operational tooling**

I owned product development for the internal tooling that ran partner operations end to end: onboarding, contract terms, SLA monitoring, and invoice verification. The goal was to replace manual, spreadsheet-driven processes with systems that could scale alongside a fast-growing partner network.

* **Defined SLA monitoring logic and exception handling** for enterprise partner accounts, so breaches and edge cases surfaced automatically instead of getting caught downstream.
* **Streamlined invoice verification**, cutting the manual checks that slowed down the settlement pipeline.

**2. Logistics order management system**

In parallel, I led requirements definition and delivery for the order management system — the backbone handling shipment transactions and financial settlements between shippers and transporters.

* Worked across **operations, finance, engineering, and commercial** teams to translate manual, tribal-knowledge workflows into concrete system specifications.
* Managed backlog prioritization and sprint planning across multiple concurrent internal workstreams, balancing operational urgency against longer-term platform needs.

**3. Scaling onboarding without scaling headcount**

As the partner network grew past 1,000 enterprise partners, onboarding itself became a bottleneck. Building internal tooling around this process — rather than adding more manual review — was what ultimately cut onboarding time from 1.5 months to under 1 month.
