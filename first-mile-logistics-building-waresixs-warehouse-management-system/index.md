Waresix had scaled fast — one of Indonesia's largest first-mile logistics SaaS platforms, serving 1,000+ enterprise partners. The internal machinery running that network hadn't scaled with it.

Partner onboarding, contract terms, SLA monitoring, invoice verification — all still largely manual, built for a partner network a fraction of the current size. There was no unified system for order transactions either, or for the multi-party financial settlements between shippers and transporters; every settlement was its own manual reconciliation exercise. Growth was starting to outpace operations.

As Senior Product Manager, I took ownership of the internal operational tooling directly — defining SLA monitoring logic and exception handling so breaches surfaced automatically instead of downstream, and streamlining invoice verification to cut manual checks out of the settlement pipeline. In parallel, I led requirements definition and delivery for the order management system itself, working across operations, finance, engineering, and commercial teams to translate manual, tribal-knowledge workflows into concrete system specifications — and managed backlog prioritization across both workstreams at once.

Operational throughput rose **15%** through workflow automation, partner onboarding dropped from **1.5 months to under 1 month** across the 1,000+ partner network, and the order management system finally gave Waresix a single source of truth for shipment transactions and settlements.
